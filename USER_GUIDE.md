# EkSamadhan AI user guide

How to use each part of EkSamadhan AI, for a business owner, a staff member and the platform
admin. Screenshots of every screen are in `docs/app-screenshots/` in the submission.

## Who does what

| Role | Can |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Owner** (shown as "Tenant") | Everything for their business: channels, knowledge, team, settings, deleting history |
| **Admin** | The same, except deleting the business's history and disconnecting channels |
| **Staff** | Answer the conversations handed to them, set their own hours and status |
| **Platform admin** | The admin panel: every business on the system, its people, usage and audit log |

## Getting started (new business)

1. **Create account** on the sign-in page: your business name, your name, email and password.
2. **Welcome**: choose where your customers write to you (Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, website).
3. **Setup guide** on the Dashboard: six steps, each with how long it takes. Click any step to open
   it. The menu shows your progress (for example "Dashboard 3/6").
   1. **Teach the AI about your shop**: Knowledge, "Start from a template", fill in the blanks.
   2. **Try your AI**: ask a question as a customer would and see the answer. Nothing is sent.
   3. **Connect where your customers write**: Channels has step-by-step help for each channel.
   4. **Set your working hours**: your own hours; hand-overs come to you only inside them.
   5. **Invite your team**, or choose "I work alone".
   6. **Choose how careful the AI is**: "Check answers first" is safest for the first week.
4. When all six are done you see **You are ready**.

## Inbox

- The list shows every conversation from every channel. The label says who has it:
  - **AI is handling**: the AI is answering; nothing to do.
  - **Needs staff** or **Waiting for you**: the AI handed it over. Open it and reply. While a
    person handles it, the AI stays quiet.
- Reply with text, a photo, a voice note or an emoji reaction.
- **Resolve** when the customer has what they needed. If they write again, the AI picks it up.
- The panel on the right shows the customer, the mood, a short summary, and lets you link the same
  person's conversations on other channels.
- **Spam** is kept in its own tab; mark it "Not spam" if it was a real customer.

## Knowledge

- Add what the AI may answer from: write text, upload a file (PDF or text), add a picture, or
  give a website address to read.
- **Try your AI**: ask a question and see the answer a customer would get, and which source it
  came from. If the AI would hand it over, add the missing answer.
- **Questions the AI could not answer** are listed so you can add the answers.

## Channels

- **Messenger and Instagram**: Connect, sign in with Facebook, choose your Page.
- **WhatsApp**: paste the phone number ID, business account ID and access token from Meta.
- **Website chat**: add your website's address, copy the code into your website, or use
  **Try the chat box** to test it first.
- "How to connect a channel" at the top has numbered steps for each.

## Team and hours

- **Team**: invite by email as staff or admin; the invite link can also be copied or shown as a QR
  code. The owner can change roles and remove people.
- **Hours**: your own weekly hours. Your status at the top (Available or Busy) is about right now;
  new conversations come to you only when you are Available and inside your hours.

## Analytics

How much the AI answered on its own, how fast replies went out, why conversations were handed to
people (grouped by what you can fix), customer mood, and the busiest times, for 7, 30 or 90 days.

## Settings

Business name and logo, AI replies on or off, "Check AI replies first", the messages sent when a
person takes over or an off-topic chat is closed, brand colour, dark mode, language (English or
Nepali), notifications, password, download your data, and deleting your account.

## Platform admin panel

Sign in as the platform admin to see:

- **Overview**: businesses, people, messages, what needs attention, and whether the services are up.
- **Businesses**: create, edit, suspend (with a reason), restore and delete businesses. A suspended
  business cannot sign in and gets no AI replies, but its customers' messages are still kept.
- **People**: everyone in every business; change roles, send a reset link, sign out, disable.
- **Audit log**: every admin action, with who did it and why; export as CSV.
- **Conversation visualizer**: every step the AI took on any message.

## Installing the app

On a computer or phone, use **Install app** at the top of the dashboard (or the browser's own
menu). It then opens in its own window and can show notifications.
